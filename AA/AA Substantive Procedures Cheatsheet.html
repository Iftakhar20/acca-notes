Substantive Procedures & Audit Cheatsheet

Tests of Detail  ·  Substantive Analytical  ·  Audit Reports  ·  Assertions  ·  IAS 37  ·  Exam Traps  ·  Key Standards
Key:
ToD Test of Detail — item by item, traced to source documents
SAP Substantive Analytical — build expectation, compare, investigate variance
00 When to Use ToD vs SAP
Use ToD When…
  • High risk or judgemental balance
  • Small number of high-value items
  • Specific misstatement already identified
  • Provisions, one-off transactions, related party
  • Existence or rights assertion is at risk
Use SAP When…
  • Predictable, routine, high-volume balance
  • You can build a reliable independent expectation
  • Payroll, interest, depreciation, rental income
  • Accuracy or completeness at aggregate level
  • Low to medium risk area needing efficient coverage
SAP — Always State 3 Things
  • The inputs you are using to build the expectation
  • What you compare the result to (the recorded figure)
  • What you do if a material difference is found
Weak Evidence — Never Use Alone
  • Enquiry of management — always pair with inspect or written confirmation
  • Observation — proves existence only, not valuation
  • "Compare to prior year" without independent expectation is not a SAP
  • Tracing in wrong direction loses the mark every time
01 Payroll / Wages
TypeProcedure
SAPMultiply headcount by average salary to develop an expected payroll figure; compare to the recorded wages expense and investigate any material variance.
SAPCompare total payroll expense to prior year; obtain management explanation for any significant increase and assess whether it is consistent with known headcount or pay changes.
ToDFor a sample of employees, agree gross pay per payroll records to contracted salary in HR files to confirm accuracy.
ToDTrace a sample of net pay amounts from payroll records to bank transfer listings to confirm payments were made to real employees and not fictitious ones.
ToDFor hourly-paid staff, recalculate gross pay by multiplying authorised hours on the timesheet by the hourly rate and agree to payroll records.
ToDAgree total payroll per the payroll records to the wages and salaries expense in the income statement to confirm completeness.
ToDReview the payroll listing before and after year end for unusual joiners, leavers, or salary changes that may indicate fictitious employees or unauthorised increases.
02 Trade Receivables
TypeProcedure
SAPCalculate receivables days for current and prior year; investigate any significant increase that is inconsistent with the company's stated credit terms.
ToDSend positive confirmation letters to a sample of customers and obtain direct written confirmation of their year-end balances — tests existence.
ToDFor balances where no confirmation is received, review post year-end cash receipts to confirm subsequent payment has been made.
ToDReview the aged receivables listing; for balances overdue beyond credit terms, assess recoverability and whether a write-down is required.
ToDFor customers in financial difficulty or administration, inspect post year-end correspondence and the administrator's report to assess the expected recovery amount.
ToDObtain management's ECL provision calculation, reperform it, and assess whether the methodology and inputs comply with IFRS 9.
03 Inventory
TypeProcedure
SAPCalculate inventory holding days for current and prior year; investigate significant increases which may indicate slow-moving or obsolete inventory.
SAPCompare gross profit margin to prior year; an unexplained fall may indicate inventory is recorded above NRV or cost of sales is overstated.
ToDFor a sample of inventory items, agree the cost per unit to supplier purchase invoices to confirm items are recorded at cost in line with IAS 2.
ToDFor slow-moving or aged items, compare cost to post year-end selling price less costs to sell to confirm inventory is held at the lower of cost and NRV.
ToDReview count sheets from the physical inventory count and trace a sample of items to the final inventory listing to confirm completeness.
ToDFor items with expiry dates, physically inspect the dates on a sample of products and assess whether any require write-down or write-off.
ToDFor unlabelled or unidentified items noted at the count, trace to purchase records or GRNs to confirm they belong to the company and are correctly valued.
04 Trade Payables & Accruals
TypeProcedure
SAPCalculate payables days for current and prior year; investigate an unexpected decrease which may indicate understatement of payables — completeness risk.
ToDObtain a sample of supplier statements and reconcile to the payables ledger; investigate items on the supplier statement that do not appear in the ledger.
ToDReview purchase invoices received after year end; confirm whether the related goods were received before year end and a payable or GRNI accrual has been recorded.
ToDFor a sample of GRNs dated before year end, confirm the related invoice or GRNI accrual is recorded in the correct accounting period — tests cut-off.
ToDReview post year-end bank payments and trace back to confirm the corresponding payable was included in year-end balances.
ToDInspect and reperform any correcting journal entries to the payables ledger; confirm each journal is properly authorised and mathematically correct.
05 Revenue
TypeProcedure
SAPCompare revenue by product line to prior year; develop an expectation and investigate any line that is materially above or below that expectation without explanation.
SAPCompare revenue by store to prior year on a per-store basis; for any new store, build an expectation based on average revenue pro-rated for the months it was open.
SAPCalculate overall revenue growth as a percentage and assess whether it is consistent with known factors such as new stores, new products, or price changes.
ToDFor a sample of sales transactions around year end, agree the date of delivery to the sales invoice to confirm revenue is recorded in the correct period — tests cut-off.
ToDFor a sample of credit notes issued after year end, trace back to assess whether the related revenue should have been reversed at year end.
ToDFor long-term contracts, agree the stage of completion and estimated total costs to supporting documentation; recalculate revenue recognised and compare to the recorded amount.
06 Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE)
TypeProcedure
SAPPerform a proof in total of the depreciation charge by applying the rate and useful life to the carrying amount, adjusted for the period in use; compare to the recorded charge and investigate variances.
ToDFor a sample of additions, agree the cost to supplier invoices and confirm the item meets the IAS 16 capitalisation criteria — future economic benefits and control by the entity.
ToDInspect invoices for capitalised items and confirm that maintenance costs, staff training costs, and ongoing service fees have been correctly expensed rather than capitalised.
ToDFor a sample of disposals, agree the proceeds to bank statements and recalculate the profit or loss on disposal; confirm it is correctly presented in the income statement.
ToDPhysically inspect a sample of PPE items to confirm they exist, are in working condition, and are still in use by the company at year end.
ToDFor revalued assets, assess the independence and competence of the valuer and agree the revalued amount to the formal valuation report.
07 Provisions & Contingencies
TypeProcedure
ToDObtain written confirmation from the company's lawyers on the probability of the claim succeeding and their estimate of the total liability amount.
ToDInspect individual claims received from claimants and agree the amounts to the provision schedule to assess whether the total provision is reasonable.
ToDReview post year-end legal correspondence and any court developments to assess whether they support or contradict the provision amount recorded.
ToDInspect board minutes for evidence that management have considered all known claims and that the provision methodology is consistent with IAS 37.
ToDConfirm whether IAS 37 criteria are met — present obligation, probable outflow, reliable estimate — or whether contingent liability disclosure is more appropriate.
ToDFor post year-end settlements actually paid, agree amounts to bank statements and use these to benchmark the reasonableness of the year-end provision.
08 Interest Expense & Bank Loans
TypeProcedure
SAPPerform a proof in total: multiply each loan balance by its interest rate and the applicable time period; compare expected interest to the recorded charge and investigate any material variance.
ToDAgree the interest rate, loan balance, and repayment terms to the signed loan agreement and confirm interest has been correctly calculated and charged in the right period.
ToDObtain a direct bank confirmation letter confirming year-end loan balances, facilities, interest rates, and any security or charges held over company assets.
ToDReview loan covenant requirements; recalculate the relevant ratios (e.g. interest cover) from the draft financial statements and assess whether any covenant breach exists.
ToDConfirm accrued interest at year end by calculating the amount outstanding from the last payment date to year end and agree to the accruals balance in the ledger.
09 Bank & Cash
TypeProcedure
ToDObtain the bank reconciliation and reperform it; agree the balance per the reconciliation to the year-end bank statement and to the cash book.
ToDObtain a direct bank confirmation letter from the bank confirming year-end balances, overdrafts, loans, and any security or charges over company assets.
ToDInvestigate any long-outstanding reconciling items in the bank reconciliation and obtain management explanation; assess whether any adjustment is required.
ToDReview post year-end bank statements for any unusual or large payments or receipts that may indicate year-end balances are misstated.
10 Proof in Total — Quick Formulas
Payroll
Headcount × Average Salary
= Expected Wages Expense

Compare to recorded wages; investigate if variance is material and unexplained.

Interest Expense
Loan Balance × Rate × (Months / 12)
= Expected Interest

Use the exact months the loan was active in the year. Compare to P&L charge.

Depreciation
Cost ÷ Useful Life × (Months in use / 12)
= Expected Depreciation

Always check the asset's start date — full year vs part year is a common trap.

Rental Income
No. of Properties × Annual Rent
= Expected Rental Income

Adjust for any known vacancies or rent-free periods during the year.

Revenue (Retail)
Existing Stores × Avg Revenue per Store
+ New Store × (Months open / 12)

Build the expectation first; then compare to the recorded total revenue figure.

VAT / Sales Tax
Revenue × Tax Rate
= Expected Tax Liability

Compare to balance on VAT return; investigate any unexplained gap.

11 Financial Statement Assertions
Occurrence

Recorded transactions actually happened and relate to the entity.

Risk: Revenue recorded for goods never delivered.

Completeness

All transactions and balances that should be recorded are included.

Risk: Payables understated — invoices not yet processed.

Accuracy

Amounts are recorded at correct values without arithmetical errors.

Risk: Depreciation miscalculated due to wrong rate applied.

Cut-off

Transactions are recorded in the correct accounting period.

Risk: Revenue for December delivery recorded in January.

Valuation

Assets, liabilities, income and expenses are at appropriate amounts.

Risk: Inventory above NRV; receivables not written down.

Existence

Assets and liabilities actually exist at the balance sheet date.

Risk: PPE on ledger was scrapped but not removed from books.

Rights & Obligations

Entity holds rights to assets and obligations for liabilities recorded.

Risk: Leased asset recorded as owned PPE.

Classification

Items are disclosed in the correct category in the financial statements.

Risk: Long-term loan not reclassified to current on covenant breach.

Presentation

Items are appropriately described and disclosed in the financial statements.

Risk: Contingent liability not disclosed in the notes.

12 IAS 37 — Provisions vs Contingencies
Probable (>50%)

Recognise a provision in the financial statements.

Must have: present obligation + probable outflow + reliable estimate of amount.

Possible (not probable)

Disclose as a contingent liability in the notes.

Do not recognise on the face of the statements. Describe nature and estimate if possible.

Remote (<5%)

No action required.

No provision, no note in the financial statements. Do nothing.

13 Audit Report — Decision Guide
✓ Unmodified Opinion

When: Financial statements give a true and fair view. No material misstatements found.

Extra sections? Going concern (if disclosed adequately), or KAM for listed entities only.

~ Emphasis of Matter

When: A matter is properly disclosed in the financial statements but the auditor wants to draw users' attention to it.

Opinion stays unmodified. Not used for disagreements or misstatements — this is the most common exam trap.

! Qualified — Except For

When: Material misstatement OR limitation on scope — but the effect is not pervasive to the financial statements overall.

Disagreement: Provision understated and management refuses to adjust.

Limitation: Auditor unable to obtain evidence over one specific balance.

✗ Adverse Opinion

When: Misstatement is both material AND pervasive to the financial statements.

Meaning: The financial statements do not give a true and fair view overall.

— Disclaimer of Opinion

When: Limitation on scope that is material AND pervasive — auditor cannot form any opinion at all.

Example: Appointed after year end with no accounting records available.

14 Materiality — Quick Benchmarks
BenchmarkTypical RangeWhen Used
Profit before tax5% – 10%Most profit-making entities — most common benchmark in AA exam questions.
Revenue0.5% – 1%Where profit is volatile, very low, or the entity is loss-making.
Total assets1% – 2%Asset-heavy entities such as banks or property companies.
Performance materiality50% – 75%Set lower than overall materiality to reduce risk of aggregate misstatements exceeding the threshold.
15 Exam Traps — Know These Cold
  • Wrong direction of testing: Completeness = go from outside the ledger into the ledger (GRN → ledger). Occurrence = go from ledger back to source document (ledger → invoice).
  • Enquiry alone scores poorly: Always pair enquiry with "inspect," "obtain written confirmation," or "recalculate." Enquiry is the weakest form of audit evidence.
  • Emphasis of Matter ≠ disagreement: EoM is only for undisputed, properly disclosed matters. Management refusing to adjust = qualified or adverse. Never EoM.
  • Wrong assertion for the risk: Fictitious transactions = occurrence (not completeness). Missing transactions = completeness (not occurrence). Always state the direction of misstatement.
  • Depreciation direction error: Full year charged on a half-year asset → depreciation overstated → PPE carrying amount understated. State the direction clearly every time.
  • "Compare to prior year" is not a SAP: A proper SAP requires building an independent expectation first. Simply comparing two recorded numbers is insufficient.
  • Too few procedures: 5-mark question = minimum 5 clear, specific, scenario-linked points. Do not write 2 procedures for a 5-mark requirement.
  • Wrong IAS reference: Provisions = IAS 37. PPE = IAS 16. Inventory = IAS 2. Impairment = IAS 36. Revenue = IFRS 15. ECL = IFRS 9. Do not mix these up.
  • Importing facts from another question: Before writing each procedure, ask "is this fact actually in this scenario?" Do not carry over details from earlier practice questions.
  • SAP-only question answered with ToD: If the requirement says "substantive analytical procedures," writing "select a sample and vouch to invoices" scores zero — that is a test of detail.
16 Key Standards — Quick Reference
StandardTopicKey Rule to Remember
IAS 2InventoryLower of cost and NRV. FIFO or weighted average only — LIFO is not permitted.
IAS 16PPECapitalise purchase price plus directly attributable costs. Expense maintenance, training, and ongoing service fees.
IAS 36ImpairmentRecoverable amount = higher of FV less costs to sell and value in use. Write down if carrying amount exceeds this.
IAS 37ProvisionsProbable + present obligation + reliable estimate = provision. Possible = disclose. Remote = nothing.
IFRS 9Financial InstrumentsECL model — recognise expected credit losses on trade receivables from day one, not only when a loss event occurs.
IFRS 15RevenueRecognise revenue when (or as) each performance obligation is satisfied. Follow the 5-step model.
ISA 240FraudAuditor responsible for detecting material misstatement due to fraud. Must discuss susceptibility with the full audit team.
ISA 315Risk AssessmentIdentify and assess risks of material misstatement through understanding the entity, its environment, and internal controls.
ISA 330Responses to RiskDesign and perform further procedures — tests of controls and/or substantive — in response to assessed risks.
ISA 570Going ConcernEvaluate management's going concern assessment; consider events and conditions up to the date of the auditor's report.
ISA 701KAMCommunicate key audit matters for listed entities only. Matters of most significance in the current period audit.
